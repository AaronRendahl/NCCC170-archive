Data Files for Selected Examples

Data files from the generalized linear mixed model book are provided here to allow readers to recreate the analyses of the examples in the book. All of them are text files. The first few lines of each file provide information on its contents. No SAS coding has been provided. Some files contain modified versions or subsets of data that were used in publications. The modifications were made to enable us to illustrate specific concepts and issues. As stated in the book, they are not intended to be the basis for drawing subject matter conclusions but rather to demonstrate the wide range of research settings to which generalized linear mixed models can be applied. A few files are part of ongoing research projects and/or manuscripts under consideration for publication. Additional files will be added as they become available.
