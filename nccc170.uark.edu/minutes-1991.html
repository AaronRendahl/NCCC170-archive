NCR-170: RESEARCH ADVANCES IN AGRICULTURAL STATISTICS

Meeting held on April 28, 1991 at Manhattan, KS

Present: Ed Gbur (AR), Linda Young (NE), Dave Marx (NE), Gary Krause (MO), George Milliken (KS), Max Rothschild (Administrative Advisor,IA), John Meadows (CSRS)

Absent: W. Lee Tucker (SD), Wyman Nyquist (IN), George Gertner (IL), Erik Nordheim (WI), W. P. Bryd (SC), Won W. Koo (ND), David Cox (IA), Paul Hinz (IA)

1990-91 Officers: George A. Milliken, Chairperson and Secretary

1991-92 Officers: Linda Young, Chairperson; Ed Gbur, Secretary

Next meeting: July 9-10, 1992 at U. Nebraska, Lincoln

This was the organizational meeting of the NCR-170 committee to establish goals and directions for the committee and for meetings of the committee.

There were two topics discussed as possible directions, On Farm Trials and Statistics in High Tech Research. It was decided to use these two topics for the next meeting. UNL volunteered to host the 1991-1992 meeting, scheduled for July 9-10, 1992. Linda Young said that she would be the chairperson and would guide the organization of the committee during 1991-1992 leading up to the meeting at UNL.

It was decided that each group would encourage students to participate in the sessions at committee meetings with the intent of developing interest in applying statistics to solve problems in agriculture.

As a future project, the publishing of a refereed journal in applied statistics was discussed with the possibility of developing such a journal as a regional publication. That possibility will be discussed again at later meetings.

Ed Gbur was selected to be the secretary for the 1991-1992 committee. G. Milliken will send lists of designated members and copies of the NCR-170 proposal to Linda Young and Ed Gbur.
